Our Story

The Cloth Craft is a brand of Powerhouz Clothing and Garments Co. LLC, a company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

The Cloth Craft is one of the best premium manufacturer wholesaler and supplier of T shirts, Polo T shirts, Hoodies, Sweatshirts ready clothing garments and fabrics in Dubai UAE and the middle east.

We at The Cloth Craft are passionate about garments, clothing, fashion and sustainable T shirts, Polo T Shirts and Hoodies and fabrics. We aim to develop premium and sustainable products. Our products are blanks in solid colors for easy printing and customization. Our products include T Shirts, Hoodies, Sweatshirts, Polo T Shirts, Joggers and Shorts. Our products come in various styles, sizes and over 20 colors. We sell primarily to business customers who offer value-added services such as embroidery, print and embellishment. Our main customers are wholesalers, suppliers, clothing brands, printers, event planners, marketing companies and corporates. We aim to give them access to our wide premium product portfolio of T shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Sweatshirts. We quickly meet the demand of our customers by providing immediate delivery of T Shirts, Polo T shirts and Hoodies and Fabrics and low MOQ. We keep developing and manufacturing new product categories, styles and colors keeping in line with the trends in the global fashion industry.

We have stock available of readymade blank plain T Shirts, Polo T shirts, Hoodies clothing and garments in Dubai UAE. Products are in solid colors, come with tear away label and have great printability for customization for any brand or corporate. We can supply products across the world to Dubai, India, Bangladesh, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Sharjah, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, United States of America (USA), London, Paris, Germany, France, Italy, Portugal and Africa. We are expanding our distributor network across GCC and Europe.

For our business clients, we also supply premium knit fabric: Single Jersey 100% organic cotton, Single Jersey 100% cotton, Single Jersey recycled cotton, Fleece and French Terry and Pique. We work across various fabric ranges. Single Jersey is available in 160 GSM, 180 GSM 220 GSM and 240 GSM. Fleece is available in 280 GSM, 300 GSM and 330 GSM in different compositions (100% cotton to mix of Cotton and Polyester). French terry is available in 280 GSM, 300 GSM, 320 GSM and 350 GSM and we can customize to can go up to premium 500 GSM French Terry.

We source our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics from manufacturers in Bangladesh, India and China. Our products are available in warehouse in Dubai and FOB delivery from India and Bangladesh. We work with manufacturers and suppliers who have quality certifications and with whom we have a long term relationship. By having our own representatives in the production country, we ensure quality in the manufacturing and logistics. We have stringent quality control of our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and fabrics before delivery to ensure consistent supplies. We use reputed third-party testing companies to test the quality of our products.

We create value with T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies that are beautiful, ethical and high quality throughout the life cycle. We adopt a thorough approach at every stage of the process from watching the fashion trends, design and sourcing to manufacturing, quality control, logistics and sales. Our philosophy is Continuous Improvement and developing new ways of doing things.

Our T shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics are premium and luxurious and provide an ideal printing surface. Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics also include Ultra Soft peached feel. Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics have high quality finish, wide color palette (Pantone), tailored style for men and women. When value is key and you want to stay simple without compromising quality our products are for you.

Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics are very printable for all types of printing Silk Screen Printing, DTG, Sublimation & Heat Transfer. For unbeatable printability cotton is ring spun and combed. Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics are tested for standard printing techniques applicable on cotton and provide easy branding solution. It’s very easy to customize thanks to no label solution.

Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics collection is structured to cover needs of clothing brands, printing companies, corporates, event planners and marketing companies. Our T Shirts Polo T Shirts and Hoodies have modern styling, a very soft silky handy and high-quality printability. We work with high quality combed cotton, ring spun cotton and pre-shrunk cotton to make the best quality fabrics. Our T Shirts, Polo T Shirts, Hoodies and Fabrics offers softness, comfort, style and premium printing possibility.

Our Polo T Shirts collection has contemporary fit, incredible colors, ultra soft and organic Pique fabric. Polo T shirts 100% pre-shrunk ring spun cotton. We work with our suppliers to continuously improve quality and work towards our sustainability commitment. Sustainability is integrated into our culture and values.

We are continuously working to create environment friendly products to reduce our carbon footprint and to protect the planet.
