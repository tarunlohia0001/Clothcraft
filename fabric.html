Organic Cotton

Each time you buy an organic cotton t-shirt instead of conventional cotton one you are reducing water consumption by 91% (397 Liters of water). Organic cotton is grown within a rotation system that builds soil fertility protects biodiversity and is grown without use of synthetic fertilizer, toxic pesticides and GMOs genetically modified objects. Organic cotton grown without chemical pesticides and using much less water, eliminating highly toxic substances from the environment. This has long-term benefits for both humanity and the planet, which is why it is often seen as more environmentally friendly compared to conventional cotton.

Recycled Cotton

Cotton waste from the manufacturing process is recycled back into cotton which can be used in apparel production. The offcuts are sorted into different colours and shredded down, then finely spun into yarn to make clothing. Thus, turning a ‘waste’ material back into something usable is a great sustainable practice. We have a range of recycled cotton products and are continuously working to increase the product range.

Sustainable Material

We are working with innovative companies that are providing sustainable and eco-friendly solutions to conserve our planet. We have a range of products made by from recycled PET fiber. Using patented technology, single use plastic PET bottle fragments are converted into fibrous re cycled PET fiber. This fiber is used to make products used in daily life.
